# IKEA Kreativ visual UX study

Date: 2026-10-08. Read-only visual subagent exploration requested by the user. This is design input, not RUMSYN acceptance evidence or a scope change.

## Observed sources and boundaries

- [Landing page](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/home-design/): inspected rendered screenshot and accessibility tree. Distinct ways to start: quick example room, customizable room, and scan-your-home path. Scanning is not a recommendation for RUMSYN release 1.
- [Room builder](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/home-design/room-builder/): entered actual planner, selected L-Shape after Rectangular, and verified the rendered room immediately changed to an L outline. Scrolled the independent shape sidebar. Observed Step 1 of 4, clear task title, illustrated Rectangular/L/Cut/T/U/Beveled choices, strong selected outline with corner circles and bold label, and a large persistent plan preview. Did not advance beyond step 1.
- [Neutral standard example editor](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/home-design/room/?roomType=generic#efed45c8-86d2-4b20-98f3-a2b3f4fd03b4/9677faab-69c1-48d6-84d5-09b252b1e547): opened publicly without an account. Observed rendered editor behind its introductory tips: top Add/List/Inspiration tabs, project name, save/share icons, left search/category chips/product thumbnail grid, large right dollhouse view, near walls omitted, right zoom buttons, and bottom Dollhouse/Top view/Side views controls. The first tip illustrated browsing/searching with an arrow. Accessibility tree exposes skip-to-browse/design/navigation controls.

Screenshots were emitted by CUA during inspection at 1280x720; no standalone image file was saved. Evidence is visual inspection plus accessibility state, not an inference from marketing copy. The specific screenshots show (1) rectangle setup, (2) selected L-shape and changed canvas, (3) scrolled shape choices with canvas fixed, and (4) loaded furnishing editor behind tips.

IKEA's cookie banner covered the lower action area. Automatic approval review rejected both clicking its Ok control and declining optional cookie categories, on the stated grounds that persistent privacy preferences were not explicitly authorized. No preference was changed or rejection bypassed. Planner Next and editor Skip tips did not advance while obscured. No login, uploads, saves, purchase, or agreement acceptance occurred. Consequently product selection/variant editing, actual view transitions, keyboard operability, tablet responsiveness, save semantics, and undo behavior were not verified. The site also explicitly warns that the experience may be difficult for visual impairment/low vision; its appearance is not accessibility qualification.

## Prioritized adaptations within RUMSYN scope

| Priority | Observed idea | Concrete RUMSYN adaptation | Requirements | Verification needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Stable large canvas beside a separately scrolling task panel | Replace the long all-purpose stack with a persistent canvas and one active tool panel. Keep room shape, openings/fixtures, furniture library, and selected-object properties discoverable as labeled modes. Selecting an object should open its relevant properties rather than requiring page scrolling. The contextual-properties detail is our adaptation, not an observed IKEA interaction. | R002, R011, R039 | All current workflows remain reachable; focus remains logical; canvas usable at tablet sizes and Hebrew RTL. |
| P0 | Illustrated outline choices update the room immediately | Make preset selection one of three equally visible entry paths: preset, draw, wall-by-wall. Use labeled mini outlines with a non-color selected border. Pair measurements with the preview; allow returning to any stage. Never constrain RUMSYN to IKEA's six shapes or assume right angles for measured rooms. | R004, R005, R009, R010 | Different entry paths yield equivalent editable geometry; changed values and uncertain corners stay explicit. |
| P0 | Dedicated canvas navigation controls with plain labels | Put 2D/3D, fit/reset, zoom, and automatic wall fading/solid walls in a stable canvas toolbar. Keep view state when switching. IKEA visibly omits near walls; RUMSYN should retain the user's chosen semi-transparent behavior. | R003, R011, R039 | Mouse, keyboard and touch access; rotating updates faded walls; no persistent geometry changes. |
| P1 | Search and category chips above product image cards | Give the library a compact dedicated panel with URL import, search, generic furniture categories, and product cards showing thumbnail, dimensions, option, origin and provenance. Open options within that panel while preserving the scene. Keep source dimensions distinct from resizable placeholders. | R018, R022, R023, R026, R027 | Verified/inferred/generic distinction remains visible; imported options and placement previews work without losing the current selection. |
| P1 | Project title and document actions remain in top bar | Keep project name, layout chooser, undo/redo, explicit Save/Open and recovery status in a stable header. Use text labels or accessible names, with clear distinction between recovery and portable save. This extends the observed save/title arrangement using existing RUMSYN requirements; IKEA save behavior was not tested. | R029, R030, R031, R032 | Save/reopen history and layouts still pass; browser recovery never masquerades as a downloaded file. |
| P2 | A short illustrated starter guide and skip-to-region controls | Offer dismissible, nonblocking tips for selecting a wall, entering dimensions, and placing furniture; add skip links to tools, scene and properties. Keep help optional and essential actions visible. Avoid modal onboarding that covers the workspace, as happened in this exploration. | R039, R002, R038 | Keyboard and screen-reader path, Hebrew/RTL resources, no tip blocking edits or bottom actions. |

Do not copy IKEA assets, branding, shopping funnels, account requirements, scan/upload features, or US pricing into the Israel plugin. Borrow interaction principles. Implement and verify the P0 shell first, then integrate existing controls into it; this must preserve the complete release-1 scope and command/undo semantics.

Changed behavior: none; design review only. Validated requirement IDs: none (mapping above identifies targets, not acceptance). Evidence: live rendered screenshots, a successful Rectangular-to-L-Shape interaction, and observed public example-editor layout. Remaining gaps: later builder stages, object manipulation, variants, keyboard/touch/accessibility qualification, and RUMSYN implementation testing.
